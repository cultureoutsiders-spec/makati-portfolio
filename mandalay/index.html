Behind The Belle & Dragon

A small dark room for
whisky and smoke.

No sign on the street. You walk through the pub on Don Carlos Palanca and keep going. Inside: a humidor, a long back bar of bourbon and Scotch, low light, and a rule about phone calls. Evenings only, Monday to Saturday.

House rules

  1. 01

    Take the call outside

    No phone calls at the table. Step out to the pub or the street.

  2. 02

    Smart casual

    No shorts, no slides. It is a small room and everyone shares the air.

  3. 03

    Over 18, with ID

    Every guest. No exceptions, whatever the group.

  4. 04

    Cigars from the humidor only

    Corkage does not apply here. Cutters and light are on the house.
