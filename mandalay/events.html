The private room, or the whole floor

A separate room off the main bar seats up to ten, with its own door, its own pours, and a host for the night. For bigger groups the whole of Mandalay can be taken on a weeknight. These formats are a sample for the demo.

Private room

Up to 10 seated. Minimum spend applies, lower on weeknights. Its own humidor tray and a host who runs the pours.

Guided tasting

Five 15ml pours around a theme (Kentucky, Islay, sherry cask, Japan) with tasting notes and one cigar pairing. ~75 minutes.

Room buy-out

All 12 seats plus the private room, Monday to Thursday. Set pour package, open humidor, your own playlist kept low.
