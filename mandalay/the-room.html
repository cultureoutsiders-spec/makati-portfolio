The room

Through the pub, keep walking

Mandalay does not have its own door on the street. You come in through The Belle & Dragon at the OPL Building on Don Carlos Palanca, walk past the bar, and the room is at the back. Tell the host you have a table at Mandalay. The copy here is a placeholder for the demo.

What it is

Twelve seats, a back bar of about eighty bottles, and a walk-in humidor. It was built as a room to sit in for two hours with one good pour and one cigar, not a place to move through. The lighting is low on purpose and there is no television.

The ventilation is the reason it works: a dedicated extraction system pulls smoke up and out so the air stays clear even on a full night. It still means everyone shares the room, which is why the dress and phone rules exist.

House rules, in full

  1. 01

    Take the call outside

    No phone calls at the table. Step out to the pub or the street. Messaging is fine.

  2. 02

    Smart casual

    Collared shirt or a clean tee, closed shoes. No shorts, no slides, no sports jerseys.

  3. 03

    Over 18, with ID

    Every guest in the group, every time.

  4. 04

    Cigars from the humidor only

    No outside cigars, no corkage. Cutter and light are on the house; ask for a draw check before you settle in.

  5. 05

    Keep it low

    It is a small room. One loud table changes the night for everyone.

  6. 06

    Last pour 2:30am

    The room closes at 3. Kitchen from The Belle & Dragon runs until 1.

Reserve before you come

Walk-ins are taken if there is room, but weekends fill. A table holds for 15 minutes past the time.
