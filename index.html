 Fine-line tattoo, one artist · Coro Hotel

Ymahe Tattoo Studio

"The quiet sheet" — a restrained consultation sheet (idea, placement, size, style, session) as the signature, a healing timeline, a calm portfolio; warm white, soft graphite, a single muted terracotta, a quiet serif.

 Small-batch artisan gelato · Legazpi Village

Coco Gelato PH

"The flavour board" — a scooping-today board with a colour dot, allergen tag and sold-out state per flavour, an allergen key, cups/cones/pints and a party-tub pre-order form; warm cream, a raspberry pink, a pistachio teal, a rounded display.

 Whisky & cigar bar, a hidden room · Legazpi Village

Mandalay Whisky & Cigars

"The humidor list" — a dark humidor and pour list with a monospace gauge-and-price column, numbered house rules, and a table / private-room request form; a warm brown-black room, low brass, an oxblood second colour, a high-contrast serif.
