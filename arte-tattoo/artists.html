Artists

Pick by style

Each artist works to their own style and keeps their own books. When you book, request an artist by name. Names, styles and rates are placeholders for the demo.

Mars

fine line · ornamental · micro

Single-needle line work, delicate florals and geometry, small script. Books 2–3 weeks out.

Kib

blackwork · lettering · cover-up

Bold black fills, patterns, chicano lettering, and turning old tattoos into something you want.

Dro

traditional · neo-traditional

American traditional: heavy outlines, limited palette, built to last. Flash always available.

Ivy

realism · black & grey · portraits

Photo-real black and grey, portraits and animals. Larger pieces across sessions.

Nix

piercing · jewellery

Ears, face and body piercing with implant-grade titanium. Walk-ins welcome; see the price list.

Not sure whose style fits? Send the idea on the booking page without naming an artist and the studio will point you to the right one.
