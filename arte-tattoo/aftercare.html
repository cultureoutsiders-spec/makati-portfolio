Aftercare

The first weeks decide how it heals

Your artist gives you specifics on the day. This is the general version, a placeholder for the demo, and not a substitute for medical advice if something looks wrong.

Tattoo — do

  • Leave the wrap on as long as your artist said (2 hours, or overnight for second-skin).
  • Wash gently with clean hands and fragrance-free soap, pat dry.
  • A thin layer of the recommended balm, 2–3 times a day for the first week.
  • Loose clean clothing over it. Fresh sheets.

Tattoo — avoid

  • Pools, the sea, baths and saunas until fully healed (2–4 weeks).
  • Direct sun; once healed, always sunscreen.
  • Picking or scratching the flakes. Let them fall off.
  • The gym for the first few days if it rubs the area.

Piercing — do

  • Sterile saline spray 2–3 times a day. Nothing else.
  • Sleep off it; a travel pillow helps for ear piercings.
  • Come back for a downsize once the swelling settles (free with our jewellery).

Piercing — avoid

  • Twisting or turning the jewellery.
  • Pools and other people's hands.
  • Changing the jewellery before it is healed (6–12 weeks for cartilage).

If something is wrong

Some redness, swelling and clear fluid is normal. Spreading heat, thick yellow-green discharge, or a fever is not. Message the studio with a photo, and see a doctor if it is getting worse.
