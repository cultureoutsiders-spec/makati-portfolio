How booking works

  1. Pick an artist by their style. Send your idea, reference images, placement and rough size.
  2. They reply with a quote and available dates. A non-refundable deposit holds the slot and comes off the final price.
  3. On the day: ID, a light meal beforehand, and time set aside. Big pieces are booked across sessions.
