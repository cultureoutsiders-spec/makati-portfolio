List my unitHow it works

Two ways to list

Rent it out, or sell it.

Column A

Rent it out

For owners who want a tenant, short or long term.

  • Photos, floor plan and building amenities on one page
  • Monthly rate, furnishing and the date it is available
  • Tenant inquiries sorted as “renting” and sent to you or the admin
Column B

Sell it

For owners ready to sell to a buyer or an investor.

  • Asking price, floor area, layout and view
  • Floor plan and location map already on the page
  • Buyers usually ask for the condominium title, tax declaration and a dues clearance, so have them ready

How it works

Four buttons, top floor.

  1. Send your details

    Fill in the form below: unit number, type, and whether you want to rent, sell or both.

  2. The admin checks

    The admin office confirms the unit and owner before anything is published.

  3. Your page goes up

    Photos, price and floor plan are set on a listing beside the building information.

  4. Inquiries arrive

    Interested renters and buyers write in. You choose who to talk to.

The verification step and who publishes listings are to be set by the building administration.

List your unit

Tell us about your unit.

A few details are enough to start. Photos can follow as a shared-folder link.

Good to have ready

  • Six to ten daylight photos: living area, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, balcony or view, entry door
  • Your asking rent or price
  • Whether it is furnished, semi-furnished or empty

By sending this you agree to be contacted about your listing. Your mobile number is not shown publicly.

Owner questions

Before you send it.

Do I need to own the unit to list it?

Listings are for registered owners or their authorised representatives. The admin office confirms ownership before a listing goes live. The exact documents are to be set by the building administration.

Who sees my phone number?

Not the public. Inquiries arrive in the admin inbox first, or straight to you if you prefer, so your mobile number is not posted on the page.

Does the site take a commission?

This demo does not process payments or take a share of any rent or sale. Commercial terms are to be agreed with the building administration at launch.

Can I list a unit for rent and for sale?

Yes. Choose “Either” on the form and both the rate and the asking price show on the listing.

How do I change or remove a listing?

Send a message through the contact page with your unit number and the change. The listing is updated, or taken down once it is rented or sold.
