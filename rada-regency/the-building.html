Rada Regency · The building

A practical tower in the middle of Legazpi Village.

Built by Cityland for professionals, urban workers and families who want to live close to the Makati business district.

About & history

Cityland-built, and close to everything.

Rada Regency was developed by Cityland Development Corporation, one of the Philippines’ best-known high-rise developers, recognised for practical, durable and budget-friendly residential and office space across Metro Manila.

The tower is a mixed-use building with residential, office and commercial space, aimed at middle-income professionals, urban workers and families who want to be near the Makati Central Business District.

Rada Street sits in Legazpi Village beside San Lorenzo Village and Greenbelt. It is known for its food scene, tree-lined sidewalks and cafes, and for easy access to Legazpi Active Park and Washington SyCip Park.
