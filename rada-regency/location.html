What is around the corner.

Parks & leisure

Legazpi Active Park and Washington SyCip Park, 2–3 minutes on foot. The Legazpi Sunday Market, 3–5 minutes.

Shopping

Greenbelt 1 to 5, about 400 m. Glorietta, Landmark and SM Makati, 10–15 minutes on foot or a short drive.

Medical

Makati Medical Center, about 500 m from the tower.

Business hubs

Ayala Avenue, PBCom Tower, GT Tower and RCBC Plaza, reachable on foot through covered elevated walkways.

Getting here

On the corner of Rada and Dela Rosa.

Rada Street is known for its food scene, cafes and tree-lined sidewalks. The building has multi-level covered parking, and the ground floor has shops for everyday errands.
