Location

Everything on the list is a short walk away.

Rada Street is known for its cafes and tree-lined sidewalks. Two parks, the Sunday market, Greenbelt and Makati Medical Center all sit within a few minutes on foot.

  • Legazpi Active Park and Washington SyCip Park, 2–3 minutes
  • Legazpi Sunday Market, 3–5 minutes
  • Greenbelt 1 to 5, about 400 m
  • Makati Medical Center, about 500 m
