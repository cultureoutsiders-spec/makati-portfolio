Privacy notice

What we collect through the forms on this site, why, who sees it, and what your rights are under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Draft for the design proposal. This notice is a working draft. Items in brackets must be completed by the building administration, and a lawyer should review it, before the site goes live.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The personal data you submit is handled by the Rada Regency building administration [legal name of the condominium corporation, to be confirmed], 100 Rada St. corner Dela Rosa St., Legazpi Village, Makati City 1229. The website is built and maintained for the administration by GCCofCI Digital, which handles submissions only on the administration’s instructions.

2. What we collect

Inquiry form: your name, mobile number, email, the type of request, an optional unit number, and your message.

Owner listing form: your name, mobile number and email; your unit number, type, size, furnishing, asking rent or price and availability date; an optional link to your photos; and any notes you add.

This site does not use advertising cookies or tracking tools. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page opens.

3. Why we collect it

  • To respond to your inquiry and arrange viewings or follow-ups.
  • To check that you own the unit, or act for the owner, before a listing is published.
  • To publish the unit details you submit (photos, price, floor area, features) as a listing on this site. Your mobile number and email are not shown on the listing.
  • To route each message to the right desk and keep a record of requests.

We use your data only for these purposes. We do not sell it or use it for unrelated marketing.

4. Legal basis

We process your data with your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. You can withdraw it at any time by contacting the person named in section 8. Withdrawing consent may mean we can no longer answer your inquiry or keep your listing published.

5. Who receives your data

  • Building administration staff who handle inquiries and listings.
  • The unit owner, when you inquire about their unit, so they can reply to you.
  • Service providers that deliver the forms and email, for example the form service and the email provider [names to be confirmed at launch]. They may process data on servers outside the Philippines.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as needed for the purposes above. [retention periods to be set by the administration, for example inquiries deleted 12 months after the last contact and listings deleted when removed] After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your data is used;
  • access a copy of your data;
  • object to processing, and withdraw your consent;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • request erasure or blocking of your data;
  • data portability, where it applies;
  • claim damages if you were harmed by inaccurate, unlawful or unauthorised processing; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.

8. Contact for privacy requests

To use any of these rights, or to ask a question about this notice, contact the administration’s Data Protection Officer: [name, email and phone of the Data Protection Officer, to be confirmed]. Until one is named, call the admin direct line at +63 (2) 8856-7232.

9. Security

We take reasonable organisational and technical steps to protect your data, including spam protection on the forms and limiting access to staff who need it. No system is completely secure. If a breach that puts your rights at risk occurs, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice. The date of the latest version will appear here. Last updated: [date at launch].
