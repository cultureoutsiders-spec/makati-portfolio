Why it tastes different

Churned here

Made in small batches on site, not shipped in. What is out today was made this week.

Italian method

Less air, less fat than ice cream, served a touch warmer so the flavour reads. The chef trained in Japan and works to an Italian recipe.

Real ingredients

Sicilian pistachio, single-origin chocolate, local calamansi and ube in season. Sorbetti are water-based and dairy-free.
