Ground floor of Coro Hotel

The studio is a room off the lobby, not a shopfront on the street. Walk into the hotel on Kalayaan Avenue and it is on the ground floor. Details below are a placeholder for the demo.

Getting here

GF, Coro Hotel, 8436 Kalayaan Avenue, Barangay Poblacion, Makati. Enter through the hotel; the studio is on the ground floor. Paid parking at the hotel; ride-hail drops you at the entrance.

Sun–Thu 1pm–10pm · Fri–Sat 1pm–12am

Before you come

Bring

Valid ID, a light meal beforehand, water. Wear something that gives easy access to the placement.

Walk-ins

Small, simple pieces only, taken when the artist is free. Message first if you can; it saves a wasted trip.

Not today if

You are unwell, sunburnt on the area, pregnant, or have had alcohol. Reschedule; it heals better.
