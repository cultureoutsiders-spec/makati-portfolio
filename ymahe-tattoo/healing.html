Healing

Thin lines need a light touch

Fine-line work heals fast but the lines are delicate while it does. Your artist gives you specifics on the day; this is the general timeline, a placeholder for the demo, and not medical advice.

  • Day 0–1Wrap stays on as instructed. When it comes off, wash once with clean hands and fragrance-free soap, pat dry, air a while.
  • Day 1–5Wash twice a day, then a very thin layer of the recommended balm. Less is more; a heavy layer suffocates fine lines. Some clear fluid and slight redness is normal.
  • Day 5–14Light flaking and itch. Do not pick or scratch. Switch to a plain fragrance-free lotion once flaking starts.
  • Week 2–4Surface healed. It may look faint or patchy under the new skin; this settles.
  • Week 4–6Final look. If a line has dropped out or gone light, book a free touch-up.

Avoid until healed

Pools, the sea, baths, saunas, direct sun, and anything that soaks or rubs the area. Once healed, sunscreen every time to keep thin lines from fading.

Touch-ups

One touch-up within three months is included. Fine lines naturally soften over years; a refresh every few years keeps them crisp.

If something is wrong

Spreading heat, thick discoloured discharge, or a fever is not normal. Message the studio with a photo and see a doctor if it worsens.
